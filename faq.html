How should I prep my hair before my appointment? +

Arrive with clean, detangled, blow-dried (or stretched) hair whenever possible — it cuts down your time in the chair and helps the style lay flatter. If you need a wash first, let us know when booking so we can build in extra time.

How long does an appointment take? +

It depends on the style, length and size — smaller braids and longer hair take more time. Most full-head styles run 3–6 hours. We'll give you a more exact estimate when you book based on the style you choose.

How long do braids typically last? +

Most protective styles last 4–8 weeks with proper care — wrapping at night, moisturizing your scalp, and avoiding excess tension. We'll walk you through aftercare for your specific style before you leave.

Do I need to bring my own hair/extensions? +

Hair is included in the price for most styles. If you have a specific brand, color or texture you'd like used, you're welcome to bring your own — just mention it when you book.

Is a deposit required? +

Larger or longer styles may require a deposit to hold your appointment slot, which goes toward your total. We'll let you know at booking if your style requires one.

What's your cancellation policy? +

Please give at least 24 hours' notice if you need to cancel or reschedule. Late cancellations or no-shows may forfeit any deposit paid.

Do you style children's hair? +

Yes — we offer kid-friendly styles and pricing. Please mention your child's age when booking so we can plan enough time and take extra care with tension.

What payment methods do you accept? +

We accept cash, card, and mobile payment (Cash App, Zelle, etc.). Payment is due at the end of your appointment unless a deposit was already collected.

Send us a message and we'll get back to you within a day.
